# Hacknight Facilitator Runsheet

## Role overview

The hacknight facilitator has three jobs each week: update the slides, MC the night and keep it on schedule, and make sure every newcomer lands in a project group.

| Duty | When | What done looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Update the slides | Before hacknight | This week's date, agenda, announcements and project list are in the Canva deck |
| MC and keep time | During hacknight | Each block starts and ends roughly on time; the room knows what's next |

## One-time access setup

You need Canva access to edit the slides. The Canva login lives in the CTWR Vaultwarden, and you request Vaultwarden access in `#organizing` on Slack. Do these in order, at least a few days before your first night.

1. **Join the CTWR Slack.** Join with the [CTWR Slack invite link](https://join.slack.com/t/civictechwr/shared_invite/zt-2hk4c93hv-DEIbxR_z1xKj8cZmayVHTw).
2. **Join `#organizing`.** It may be private, so ask an organizer to add you.
3. **Request Vaultwarden access in `#organizing` by tagging `@access-admins`.** Post something like: "Hi! I'm facilitating hacknight starting [date]. @access-admins, could you invite me to the CTWR Vaultwarden so I can get the Canva login?"
4. **Accept the Vaultwarden invite.** Create your account and master password. Store the master password somewhere safe, because it can't be recovered.
5. **Get the Canva credentials from Vaultwarden** and sign in to Canva.
6. **Open the hacknight slide deck** ([Canva link](https://www.canva.com/design/DAHBmv8wbh0/v98DKjauHI7MI2UVvJdzfg/edit)) and bookmark it.

**Ensure you have checked off each of these items:**

- On CTWR Slack
- In `#organizing`
- Vaultwarden account working
- Signed in to Canva and found the deck

Never paste the Canva password into Slack or share it outside Vaultwarden.

## Prep timeline

Hack night is Wednesday, and the slides should be done by Monday. Ideally, collect next week's announcements during the current hack night, so there's little left to chase.

| Day | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Sunday | **Fill the gaps.** Check replies to the Slackbot thread in `#organizing` and chase gaps |
| Monday | **Slides done.** Update the Canva deck and post the link in `#organizing` for review |
| Tuesday | **Final check.** Apply feedback, run through in presenter mode, confirm the venue |
| **Wednesday** | **Hack night.** Run the night, 5:45 to 8:00 PM, and note next week's announcements at recap |
| Thursday | **Start next week's list.** Slackbot asks `#organizing` for updates at 10am |
| Friday | Nothing to do |
| Saturday | Nothing to do |

If it's your first time facilitating, do the one-time access setup at least a week ahead. Vaultwarden access waits on someone in `#organizing`, so that step can't be rushed the night before.

## Weekly prep: update the slides

Finish the slides by Monday, so organizers have time to flag anything missing before Wednesday.

1. Duplicate last week's slides, or edit them in place. Follow whatever convention the deck already uses.
2. Check `#organizing` in Slack for this week's announcements, events and project updates. A Slackbot reminder posts there every Thursday at 10am asking for updates, and organizers reply in its thread. You don't need to send the ask yourself. Follow up on anything missing.
3. Update and check off each of these slides:
    - Title slide: this week's date (and the theme or speaker, if there is one)
    - Agenda with this week's times
    - Announcements and upcoming events (remove last week's)
    - Active projects: name, one-line pitch, who to talk to
4. Click through once in presenter mode to catch typos and stale dates.
5. Post the deck link in `#organizing` and tell people it's ready.

## Night-of run of show

Hack night runs Wednesdays from 5:45 to 8:00 PM at Builders Club.

| Time | Block | Facilitator does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before 5:45 PM | Setup | Arrive early, test the projector and slides, put up the Wi-Fi details, set out name tags |
| 5:45 – 6:00 PM | Doors open | Greet people as they grab food and settle in, and spot newcomers (see below) |
| 6:00 – 6:15 PM | Introductions and announcements | Start on time: what CTWR is, code of conduct, announcements. Newcomers say their name and what they're interested in |
| 6:15 – 6:30 PM | Project overviews | Each project lead says what they're building and who they need |
| 6:30 – 7:45 PM | Breakout groups | Join a breakout group yourself. Step out briefly to check on newcomers, and give a heads-up around 7:40 |
| 7:45 – 8:00 PM | Recap and wrap up | Each group shares what they did and their next steps. Note any announcements for next week. Thank everyone and give the next date |
| 8:00 PM – late | Optional hangout | Point people to the hangout, then tidy up before you leave |

Timekeeping tips: give a 2-minute signal before each transition, and cut project overviews off politely at time.

## Greeting and orienting newcomers

The goal: no newcomer leaves without having talked to someone and tried out a group.

1. **Greet them at the door.** Ask their name, whether it's their first time, and what brought them in.
2. **Ask about their background.** Ask what they do or want to learn (dev, design, data, research, community, project management). Non-coders are welcome, so say so.
3. **Explain the night in one sentence:** "We do introductions and project overviews, then split into groups to work until recap at 7:45."
4. **Introduce them to someone.** Pair them with a project lead or a regular before introductions start, if you can.
5. **During project overviews, point out the projects** that match what they told you.
6. **At breakout, walk them to a group.** Hand them off by name to the project lead.
7. **Check in mid-breakout.** Ask whether they're set up and have something to do. If not, move them or find them a task.
8. **Before they leave,** share the [CTWR Slack invite link](https://join.slack.com/t/civictechwr/shared_invite/zt-2hk4c93hv-DEIbxR_z1xKj8cZmayVHTw) and mention next week's date.

If several newcomers come at once, ask a regular to help, or start a quick newcomer huddle before breakout.
